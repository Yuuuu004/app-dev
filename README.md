# Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse (2023)

> *"Everyone keeps telling me how my story is supposed to go. Nah. I'm-a do my own thing."*

## Overview
**Spider-Man: Across the Spider-Verse** is a 2023 animated superhero film featuring Miles Morales. Catapulted across the Multiverse, Miles encounters a team of Spider-People charged with protecting its very existence—and must redefine what it means to be a hero when they clash on how to handle a new threat.

### What Makes It Stand Out
1. **Visual Artistry:** Every universe features a completely distinct animation style, from watercolor palettes to comic-book halftones.
2. **Soundtrack:** Blends hip-hop, electronic beats, and orchestral motifs to match each character's energy.
3. **Storytelling:** Balances high-stakes multiverse action with deeply personal family dynamics.
